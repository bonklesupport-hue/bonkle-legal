Frequently asked questions

What is this app?

A collection of very short arcade mini games. In a match you and an opponent each play the same game with the same layout, and the better score wins the round. First to two rounds takes the match.

What are coins?

A virtual currency inside the app. Matches cost nothing to enter, and every finished match pays a coin prize: 150 for a win, 75 for a draw and 38 for a loss. You spend coins on character looks and on retrying a round. Coins have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, sold or transferred, and you cannot win real money.

I ran out of coins.

You can always keep playing: matches cost no coins to enter and every match pays a prize, there is a daily bonus, and Practice needs no coins. You can also buy coin packs in the Store, but you never have to.

What does "Unlock All Games" do?

Games unlock as you play. The one-time purchase opens every game in Practice right away.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open the Store and tap Restore Purchases (also in Settings). Coin packs are consumable and cannot be restored, but coins already in your wallet stay with you, and they sync through iCloud if you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. Game Center is optional and is used for leaderboards, achievements and matches against friends.

Why can't I start a match without internet?

Matches need an internet connection. Practice works offline.

The tilt games do not work.

Tilt needs a real iPhone or iPad. Hold the device the way you play before the countdown ends, because the game calibrates to that position.

There is no sound.

The app follows your silent switch. Turn off silent mode, raise the volume, and check the sound switch in the app's Settings.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund purchases directly.

What does the app collect, and how do I delete my data?

Game progress, coins and settings stay on your device, and in your private iCloud if you use iCloud sync. Delete the app, or the iCloud data in iOS Settings, to remove them. Anonymous play statistics can be turned off in the app under Settings, "Share anonymous play stats". Your leaderboard character look can be hidden under Settings, "Show my character on leaderboards". For details or deletion requests, see the Privacy Policy or email us.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Use Send feedback in the app's Settings, or email bonkle.support@gmail.com.
